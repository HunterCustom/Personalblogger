Hi, I'm **Hunter Kovel**, the person behind HunterCustom.

I'm an enterprise IT field service professional with a growing focus on networking, systems, storage, and infrastructure, a Miata owner with a long-running project, and someone who usually has a few things apart at the same time. This site is where I keep track of what I'm building, repairing, and learning.

## My work in IT

My professional background includes enterprise hardware support with **IBM and Unisys**, working with Dell PowerEdge servers, NetApp storage, Lenovo infrastructure, Cisco networking hardware, enterprise printing, and client systems. I enjoy working through a problem, identifying the failure, coordinating the pieces needed to resolve it, and getting equipment back into service.

At IBM, I continue to build on that field experience through technical learning in enterprise storage and server platforms, including IBM FlashSystem and Storage Insights, NetApp AFF/ASA service, Lenovo ThinkSystem, SAN concepts, and server service training. I keep training and hands-on production experience clearly separated when documenting my skills.

At home, I continue learning through a Dell PowerEdge R730 running Unraid and Docker, a secondary Lenovo M900 server, a segmented UniFi network, and virtual machines. These projects give me a place to develop the networking and infrastructure-administration skills that complement my professional hardware experience.

You can find my experience, credentials, technical training, and selected projects in my [IT Portfolio]({{ '/portfolio/' | relative_url }}).

## Outside of work

A lot of my spare time goes into my **NB Miata build**, motorsports, and projects around the garage. The Miata has become a long-term lesson in planning, troubleshooting, and working through setbacks. I also enjoy derby racing, working on my jet ski, and finding practical uses for the technology I bring home.

Bri and I are preparing to move from Connecticut to **Burlington, North Carolina, in November 2026**. I'm looking forward to settling into a new home, organizing the garage, and continuing these projects there.

## Why I keep this blog

I started HunterCustom because I wanted a place to document the details that are easy to forget: why I chose a part, what I tried, what went wrong, and what finally helped.

You'll find finished work here, but you'll also find projects in progress and plans that change as I learn more. Writing those things down helps me, and I hope some of the notes are useful to someone working through a similar problem.
