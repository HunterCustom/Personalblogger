Hi, I'm **Hunter Kovel**, the person behind HunterCustom.

I'm an IT field service professional, a Miata owner with a long-running project, and someone who usually has a few things apart at the same time. This site is where I keep track of what I'm building, repairing, and learning.

## My work in IT

My background includes enterprise hardware support with **IBM and Unisys**, working with servers, storage systems, networking equipment, and client devices. I enjoy the process of working through a problem, finding the cause, and getting equipment back into service.

At home, I continue learning through a Dell PowerEdge R730 running Unraid, Docker applications, a UniFi network, and virtual machines. I'm developing my networking and infrastructure administration skills alongside the hardware experience I've gained at work.

You can find my experience, credentials, and selected projects in my [IT Portfolio]({{ '/portfolio/' | relative_url }}).

## Outside of work

A lot of my spare time goes into my **NB Miata build**, motorsports, and projects around the garage. The Miata has become a long-term lesson in planning, troubleshooting, and working through setbacks. I also enjoy derby racing, working on my jet ski, and finding practical uses for the technology I bring home.

Bri and I are preparing to move from Connecticut to **Burlington, North Carolina, in November 2026**. I'm looking forward to settling into a new home, organizing the garage, and continuing these projects there.

## Why I keep this blog

I started HunterCustom because I wanted a place to document the details that are easy to forget: why I chose a part, what I tried, what went wrong, and what finally helped.

You'll find finished work here, but you'll also find projects in progress and plans that change as I learn more. Writing those things down helps me, and I hope some of the notes are useful to someone working through a similar problem.
