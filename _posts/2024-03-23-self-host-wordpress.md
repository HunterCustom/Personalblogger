I was paying about $30 a month for Wix to host huntercustom.net, where I offered Nintendo Switch modding services. I wanted a cleaner design and lower hosting costs. I considered Shopify, then found WooCommerce, an e-commerce extension for WordPress. I already had a Lenovo M900, so I decided to try hosting the site myself.

I followed a tutorial and spent the past week rebuilding the site. As of March 23, 2024, I still need to finish the homepage and terms, and some people who visited the old Wix site are having trouble reaching the new one.

I also moved the domain from Google Domains to Cloudflare and set up email for the domain.

I want to document the rest of the move once I have more energy. A month of ten-hour shifts has worn me out, and I'm waiting to hear whether East Hartford Public Schools will invite me back for a third interview.
