The Miata's engine failed during Pat Gorman's Autumn Apex 2.0. I don't know exactly why yet. As of October 13, 2024, I haven't pulled it: I'm moving soon and don't have an engine hoist of my own.

The car is going into storage while I figure out the rebuild and how to pay for it. I'm considering a second job to help cover the cost. My goal at this point is around 300 wheel horsepower, but first I need to find out what happened inside the engine.
