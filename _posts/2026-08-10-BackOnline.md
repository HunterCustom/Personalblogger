**HunterCustom.com is back online!** After some time away, I've reacquired the domain and brought the blog back. I've missed having one place to document the projects I'm working on and the things I learn along the way.

## What I'll Be Sharing

The Miata will continue to be a big part of this site, along with my jet ski, homelab, repairs, and occasional life updates. There always seems to be another project waiting for attention, and I want to do a better job of recording the progress while the details are still fresh.

That includes the decisions behind a project: why I chose a particular part or setup, what I tried when something didn't work, and what I would change the next time around.

## A Fresh Start for the Site

Bringing the site back has also been a chance to work through a few things that bothered me before. The distracting shimmering effect I was seeing on images appears to be resolved in this version, which makes browsing the posts much nicer.

I'm looking forward to adding new updates and continuing the record of projects already covered here. Having that history in one place makes it easier to see how much has changed, even when an individual project feels like it's moving slowly.

## Keeping the Notes Useful

Some posts will be detailed technical writeups; others will be shorter progress updates. My goal is to capture enough information that I can return to a project later and understand what I did and why.

If those notes help someone else with a similar repair or build, even better. Thanks for stopping by—I'm glad to have a place to share the work again.
