My dad and I took the Accord enduro car to Waterford Speedbowl for open practice. We filled the tank on the way, but when we arrived, all we could smell was gasoline. We looked underneath and found two major leaks in the tank.

That put the enduro plan on hold. We're looking at repairing or replacing the tank and may run the car in a demolition derby instead.
