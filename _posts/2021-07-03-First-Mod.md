My first modification to the Miata was a small one: replacing the factory interior bulb with an LED. I also swapped the lights in the sun visors. I picked up the parts from [DanDak.net](https://dandak.net).

![LED interior lighting inside the Miata](/assets/img/2021/interiorlights.png)

It's a simple change, but the cabin is much easier to see at night. It also gives the interior a more modern look without changing much about the car.

![Miata cabin illuminated by the new LEDs](/assets/img/2021/brightinteriorlights.png)
