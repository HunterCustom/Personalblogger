Summer Showdown 2.0, hosted by my friend Pat Gorman, was a day when I focused more on flying the drones than driving the Miata.

## The Drones

The seven-inch drone kept desyncing near full throttle. Since chasing cars often puts me near that limit, I grounded it and switched to my five-inch backup. The five-inch drone flew much better, but most of its batteries were barely holding a charge. I only had two that would last about three minutes each.

## Driving the Miata

I bought another GoPro Hero 9 Black and set it to start recording automatically when it received USB power. It worked in my tests and then recorded absolutely nothing at the event. I still don't know why.

This was also my first time driving with racing gloves. I didn't expect to like them as much as I did. Pat designed another great, flowing course, and I wish I'd had the footage to share.
