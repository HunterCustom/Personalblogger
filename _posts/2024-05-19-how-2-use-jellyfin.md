This is a quick guide for friends and family using my Jellyfin and Jellyseerr setup. You'll need an account from me before signing in.

## Watch with Jellyfin

1. Open a browser and go to `jellyfin.huntercustom.pro`.

2. Enter the username and password I provided.
3. Sign in and browse the library to find something to watch.

---

## Request Media with Jellyseerr

1. Open `requests.huntercustom.pro` in a browser.
2. Sign in with your account.
3. Search for a movie or show.
4. Open the title and choose **Request**.
5. Check the **Requests** page to follow its status.

## If Something Goes Wrong

Double-check your login details and internet connection. If either site is down or your account stops working, let me know and I'll take a look.
