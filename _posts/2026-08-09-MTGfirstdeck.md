Bri and her family play *Magic: The Gathering* together, and they invited me to join them. Her dad helped me build my first deck around **Lathril**. I'm brand-new to the game, so I've got a lot to learn, but I'm excited to play with them.

I used to get teased for liking Pokémon, and for a long time I didn't even realize there was a whole game behind the cards. Getting into Magic with Bri's family feels like a fun way to give card games another shot.

I'll come back with the deck list and some notes once I've played a few games.
