The original wheel and dashboard looked good for a 1999 car, especially one that had been in an accident. I still wanted to give the interior a more modern, factory-style look.

## A New Steering Wheel

I found a [Cipher Auto steering wheel for the NB Miata](https://cipherauto.com/collections/steering-wheels/products/enhanced-steering-wheel-for-mazda-miata-nb-leather-with-silver-stitching) that fit what I had in mind.

![Original Mazda steering wheel](/assets/img/2021/oldsteeringwheel.png)

I expected the swap to be a major job, but most of it went smoothly. I removed the steering column cover, undid the two bolts holding the airbag, and loosened the steering wheel fastener with a 22 mm socket. The original wheel needed a few firm taps to come loose. I hit it too hard, though, and damaged the clock spring connector. That turned the wheel swap into a clock spring replacement too.

## Changing the Gauges

I also wanted to replace the factory green gauge lighting with a cooler white look. After browsing [revlimiter](https://revlimiter.net), I chose the Revolver gauges over the GT-40 set. They looked like something Mazda might have used if the car had been built later.

![New revlimiter gauges in the Miata](/assets/img/2021/revlimiter.png)

## Finishing the Interior

The new wheel and gauges looked great together, but the original airbag didn't quite match. I found an airbag in a wrecked 2003 Mazda Protegé at a local junkyard and installed it to finish the look. I have not verified whether that airbag is compatible with the Miata restraint system, so this is a record of the project, not installation guidance.
