This was the biggest maintenance job I had tackled myself, and I was learning as I went.

## Maintenance at 60,000 Miles

After a fun little drive to the abandoned mill factory, I hit 60,000 miles on my 23-year-old car. Figured it was time for some maintenance to freshen her up. Unsure of my exact plans for the car yet, I decided to prioritize reliability before any modifications.

Inspired by the latest video from the Miata Dad himself, I followed the guide he provided and purchased all the recommended parts from Treasure Coast Miata for about $550.

I'll admit, I was nervous about tackling this job. The most I'd ever DIY'd on a car before was my own oil change, and now I was taking on the timing belt, water pump, and engine seals. But where better to learn than at my mechanic job, right? Thankfully, my boss allowed me to use a spare garage bay during my lunch breaks and a few hours on Saturdays, free of charge.

![Engine Bay Taken Apart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HunterCustom/HunterCustom.github.io/master/assets/img/60k%20Maint/IMG_5881%20%282%29.jpg)

*With the front of the engine apart, I could get to the maintenance items.*

Disassembly went more smoothly than I expected. The coolant hoses came off with a razor and a spreader tool, and rust didn't slow me down.

I replaced or serviced:

- Gates water pump
- Gates timing belt kit
- OEM water tubing
- Engine oil
- Coolant
- Harmonic balancer

## Cooling Upgrades

I also installed a SuperMiata coolant reroute and CFS radiator. I wanted more cooling capacity before putting the car through harder track use; I'll see how the setup performs as I drive it.

![Engine Bay with upgrades](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HunterCustom/HunterCustom.github.io/master/assets/img/60k%20Maint/IMG_5988.jpg)

## New Wheels and Tires!

I chose bronze Konig wheels in 15 × 8, +25 offset, with Hankook RS4 tires in 225/45R15. I wanted a setup that would look good and hold up to track driving.

![New Rims mounted](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HunterCustom/HunterCustom.github.io/master/assets/img/60k%20Maint/IMG_6098.jpg)

My old employer let me mount and balance the tires myself. Getting to do that work gave me more confidence with the rest of the project.

## DIY Alignment? Not Really
