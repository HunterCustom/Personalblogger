After four or five months of work, I finally managed to get my 7-inch FPV drone up and flying . However, the battery I had was in bad shape and instantly dropped to 20 volts. Despite this setback, the drone still flew for 6 minutes, even though I had to take it easy with that battery.

The drone feels impressive in the air. With the swollen 2,600 mAh LiPo, the all-up weight is 44.5 ounces (about 1,261 grams). This includes the full weight of the drone itself, a GoPro Hero 9, and the mentioned battery.

I have a track event on April 20, and the new 5,000 mAh LiPo I ordered weighs 798 grams on its own. 

The drone and GoPro weigh 1 pound 12.1 ounces (about 797 grams), so the new battery should bring the total to roughly 1,595 grams. I'll weigh the complete setup once it arrives.

That's about a 3.5-pound FPV drone! This should be an interesting experience to fly. I might need to consider building a naked GoPro 9 to shed some weight.
