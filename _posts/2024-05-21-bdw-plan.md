## Original Plan

My first parts list for the Miata's drive-by-wire conversion was: 
- Skunk2 Intake Manifold
- Bosch 68 mm throttle body
- Bosch drive-by-wire pedal assembly
- Haltech ECU
- DIY wiring harness

## Revised Plan

- Skunk2 Intake Manifold
- Bosch 68 mm throttle body
- Haltech ECU
- DIY wiring harness
- Honda accelerator pedal position (APP) sensor

### Why a Honda APP Sensor?

I first came across the term through [Justin Barrera](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1984143951912788/user/1004109046), who documented his own drive-by-wire conversion. APP stands for accelerator pedal position.

Justin converted a 1.8-liter NB with the factory intake and chose an ND Miata throttle body. I'm planning to keep the Skunk2 intake manifold and Bosch throttle body in my version.
His older posts suggest that he considered a Mercedes pedal before switching to a Honda APP sensor. I found part number **37971-RBB-003**, priced around $150–$175 when I checked, plus an aftermarket option around $40. I still need to verify compatibility and the exact wiring before using either one.
